AUTO · box opens every 8s — one flag in, slam shut Sim speed

Fully automatic, like the live stream: there is no button to remove a country by hand. The centre box stays shut while the “Hole Opens In” counter counts down from 8. Then it opens with a loud sound and stays open until a flag rolls over it — that flag, and only that flag, is pulled in, and the box slams shut the same instant with a bang, a screen shake and the announcer voice. Five flags can never go in together: the box is already shut behind the first one. New — Final 2 Zone: when only 2 countries are left, the red zone ring starts closing in from the edge, shrinking the arena smaller and smaller and pushing both finalists towards the centre hole until one falls in. Live chat support: while the match is live, comments stream into the Live Comments panel and every comment adds support to a country on the CHAT SUPPORT board — at 20 support a country earns a Shield that saves it from the hole once, and an eliminated country that reaches 40 support is Revived back into the arena once. Use a high sim speed only to test a long round quickly.
